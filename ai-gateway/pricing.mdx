description: "How gateway calls are charged in pre.dev credits, and what the free allowance covers."

Gateway calls are paid with the same pre.dev credits as coding, planning, and browser tasks.

## How a call is priced

Each call is charged in credits from the model's metered usage for that call, at the model's credit price. The credits charged are reported in the `x-predev-credits-charged` header.

The credit price per model is listed on [`GET /v1/models`](/ai-gateway/api/models): each row carries a `predev` object with `credits_per_m_input` and `credits_per_m_output`, and, where the model has them, prices such as `credits_per_m_cache_read`, `credits_per_m_cache_write`, `credits_per_image`, and `credits_per_request`. Read those numbers rather than deriving prices yourself.

Worked example: a model whose row lists `credits_per_m_input: 20` and `credits_per_m_output: 100` costs 0.2 credits for 10,000 input tokens plus 0.2 credits for 2,000 output tokens, so a call of that size settles at about 0.4 credits.

## What is included

- Plugin costs, such as web search, are part of the call's charge.
- With `models` fallbacks, you pay the price of the model that answered.
- Streams settle when they end. If your client disconnects mid-stream, the call is charged for what the model generated.
- Video jobs are charged when a status poll reports completion. See [images and video](/ai-gateway/api/images-and-video).
- Catalog reads (`/v1/models` and the per-modality model lists) are free and not rate-limited.

Non-streaming responses report the charge in `x-predev-credits-charged` and the balance in `x-predev-credits-remaining`. [`GET /v1/usage`](/ai-gateway/api/usage) breaks the last 1 to 90 days down by model and path.

## Free allowance

A Free workspace can spend **5 credits** on gateway calls in total, whatever its credit balance. After that, every call returns `402` with code `subscription_required` until the workspace subscribes. Free workspaces are also limited to 30 requests per minute.

## Out of credits

A workspace without enough credits for a call gets `402` with code `insufficient_credits` before anything is sent to the model, so nothing is generated and nothing is charged. The error body includes `credits_remaining`, `estimated_credits`, and a `topup_url`. See [errors](/ai-gateway/api/errors).

Turn on auto-recharge in billing to keep a live app running when the balance would otherwise reach zero.

## Rate limit

Requests are limited per workspace per minute: 600 by default, 30 on the Free plan. A `429` carries a `Retry-After` header. Catalog reads do not count.

See [plans, credits, and pricing](/coding-agent/plans-and-credits) for plan allowances.
