## Rerank models

Rerank models are not returned by [`GET /v1/models`](/ai-gateway/api/models) or [`GET /v1/models/public`](/ai-gateway/api/public-models). These ids work today: `cohere/rerank-v3.5`, `cohere/rerank-4-fast`, `cohere/rerank-4-pro`, `voyageai/rerank-2.5`, `voyageai/rerank-2.5-lite`, and `qwen/qwen3-reranker-8b`.
